<section class="section" aria-labelledby="about">
  <h2 id="about">About</h2>
  <p>I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Memphis, where I direct the Data System Research (DSR) Lab. My research develops scalable systems and algorithms for evolving, distributed, and graph-structured data. I received my Ph.D. from HKUST and was a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Waterloo, CUHK, and HKUST.</p>
</section>
